QUICK TIPS
💡 Did you know a hairline crack in your foundation can double in size within one rainy season?+
Water seeps into micro-cracks, freezes and expands, then widens the gap. Seal cracks under ¼ inch yourself with hydraulic cement. Anything wider needs a pro assessment.
💡 Did you know most bathroom remodels require a permit in California?+
Moving or adding plumbing, electrical, or structural walls always requires a permit. Skipping this can block a future sale or trigger code enforcement.
💡 Did you know your driveway slope affects drainage and city compliance?+
California requires driveways to drain away from the structure. A slope of 1–2% is ideal. FXR grades every pour to spec.
💡 Did you know an ADU can increase your property value by up to 30%?+
California's ADU laws have made it easier than ever. FXR handles the full scope from permits to move-in ready.
💡 Did you know a sagging roofline usually means a compromised rafter or ridge board?+
This is a structural issue, not cosmetic. Call a contractor before your next rainstorm. Left unaddressed it can lead to rafter failure.

Code Cases & Red Flags

RED FLAG
What Is a Code Enforcement Case — And What Happens Next?
Received a notice from the city? Here's exactly what it means and what NOT to do.
A code enforcement case is opened when a city inspector or neighbor complaint triggers a review of your property. You'll receive a Notice of Violation with a correction deadline — typically 30 to 60 days. Missing this deadline escalates to fines or city-hired contractors at your expense. FXR responds fast — we pull the permits, do the corrective work, and handle the inspection process.
WARNING
What Happens If You Build Without a Permit in California?
Unpermitted work can cost you far more in the long run. Here's the real risk.
California requires permits for structural, electrical, plumbing, or mechanical work. Building without one can result in Stop-Work Orders, Red Tags, legalization costs that exceed the original project, and complications when selling. FXR has helped dozens of homeowners navigate this process cleanly.
SOLUTION
How FXR Resolves Code Violations — Step by Step
We've resolved red tags and stop-work orders across the Central Valley.
Step 1: Site Visit — assess the property and identify what work is needed. Step 2: Permit Pull — submit all required applications to the city. Step 3: Corrective Work — bring everything into compliance. Step 4: Final Inspection — coordinate with the city inspector and be on-site. Step 5: Case Closure — confirm the case is closed and provide documentation. Most cases resolved within 30–90 days.
INSIGHT
Red Tag vs. Stop-Work Order: What's the Difference?
Both are serious — but not the same. Know the difference.
A Red Tag prohibits occupancy of an unsafe structure. A Stop-Work Order halts active construction. A red tag is more serious — it can affect tenants and trigger relocation requirements. Both require immediate action. Call FXR at 408-769-9928 before responding to the city on your own.
