GoGreen Home is a State of California-backed program that makes low interest rates and extended terms available for home energy upgrades. FXR Construction is an enrolled GoGreen Home contractor.

Who Qualifies

Made For Real
California Households.

  • PG&E, SCE, SDG&E, or SoCalGas customers — that covers the Central Valley and the South Bay
  • Single-family homes, condos, townhomes, duplexes to fourplexes, and manufactured homes
  • Homeowners — and renters with the owner's written permission
  • Unsecured loans through participating lenders: no home equity or refinance needed
